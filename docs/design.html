Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Reserve gold (accent / accent-light) for one primary action or highlight per view.
  • Set all Anton and Bebas Neue text in uppercase.
  • Use hairline border tokens, not shadows, for separation.
  • Keep button/input radii in the sm–md range; lg is for containers only.
  • Pair gold buttons with on-accent (near-black) text.

Don't

  • Don't introduce a second brand color.
  • Don't add drop shadows or glassmorphism.
  • Don't set body or nav copy in Anton or Bebas Neue.
  • Don't use radii above 12px anywhere.
  • Don't let secondary buttons use the gold accent.
  • Don't lighten bg into mid-gray "dark mode" gray.
